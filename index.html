Rarity

Each generated Nutt has a rarity rank based on how often its traits appear. Rank 1 is the rarest. Specials (1/1s and honoraries) sit outside this ranking, so the site doesn’t show a rank for them. Check any Nutt’s traits and rank in My Nutts.

Stash Defense

Tap the nut thieves before they raid the stash. One of the real Nutts stands guard each game.

  • Thieves walk in from the sides and the branches. Tap them to bonk them.
  • 🥷 Ninjas are fast, 👑 bosses take 4 hits.
  • If a thief grabs nuts, tap him while he runs to win them back for bonus points.
  • Chain hits for combos up to ×5. Clear a wave for +2 nuts.
  • Golden acorns give power-ups: ❄ Freeze, 💥 Nut Bomb or +5 nuts.
  • Game over when the stash is empty. Post your score on X.
